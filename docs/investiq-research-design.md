# InvestIQ: research architecture and novelty validation

**Status:** pre-implementation research proposal, not a result or investment product
**Review cut-off:** 22 November 2026
**Target:** an auditable academic study of financial forecasting and scenario reasoning. No claim of algorithmic novelty or demonstrated predictive advantage is made.

## 1. Research question and scope

Can a point-in-time, evidence-linked financial state model, coupled to a disagreement-preserving research protocol and an immutable forecast/outcome ledger, improve *measurable* forecast quality, scenario reliability, and research accountability over strong, similarly resourced alternatives?

The initial empirical domain is **US listed nonfinancial operating companies**, quarterly revenue and daily total-return series. This is a research platform for public information, not personalized advice, execution, or a claim that a company is physically replicated. “Digital twin” is an operational label for a synchronized, versioned latent-state and simulation model of an issuer, not evidence that the model faithfully captures the company. Daily price returns and quarterly revenue are distinct tasks with distinct clocks; no frequency is imputed as if it were observed.

**Candidate contribution, conditional on experiments:** an integrated, auditable protocol connecting (i) as-of entity states, (ii) explicit scenario admissibility and uncertainty about causal assumptions, (iii) preserved agent-level dissent, and (iv) a precommitted forecast-resolution ledger. The individual techniques and many close combinations already exist. The empirical question is whether this specific combination offers incremental benefits, at what cost and under which conditions. A demonstrable method-level contribution would require a sharpened claim, a deeper prior-art search, and positive, replicable experiments; a negative result remains publishable as a systems evaluation.

## 2. Structured literature review

### 2.1 Review method and limitations

This is a **targeted, structured exploratory review**, not a systematic review or exhaustive novelty clearance. On 22 November 2026, web searches used combinations of: `financial digital twin risk simulation`, `causal counterfactual finance structural causal model partial identification`, `multi agent debate disagreement preservation forecasting`, `conformal prediction financial time series distribution shift`, `proper scoring rules forecast evaluation`, `TradingAgents`, and `point in time SEC filings restatements`. Sources were selected for direct relevance to one of the five objectives, clear methodological overlap, accessible primary text where possible, and a mix of established foundations and recent adjacent work. We inspected search excerpts and accessible article text; search ranking, coverage, source quality, and author claims limit the conclusions. No exhaustive corpus, screening counts, PRISMA flow, duplicate adjudication, or independent second reviewer was produced. Recent arXiv works are preprints, and their empirical claims are not independently validated here.

**Before any novelty claim:** preregister a fuller search in Scopus/Web of Science, Google Scholar, SSRN, arXiv, and relevant finance/ML proceedings; log exact queries, run dates, database coverage, inclusion/exclusion decisions, duplicates, citation chaining, full texts, and independent-reviewer disagreements. Search patents and concurrent work where appropriate. Update the matrix against exact mechanism and task; document null results. An inability to access a database is a limitation, not evidence of absence.

## 3. Architecture and information flow

```text
public source snapshots + licensed price/universe data
  -> timestamp/identity/unit validation -> immutable evidence & vintage store
  -> as-of dataset manifest -> latent-state estimator + structural model registry
  -> parallel independent research agents -> evidence/claim validator
  -> optional bounded debate; append original and revised positions, never overwrite
  -> scenario gate + uncertainty engine -> forecast distribution / abstention
  -> append-only issuance ledger -> outcome resolver -> immutable score events
  -> dashboards, reproducible experiment manifests, monitoring and review
```

1. **Evidence and time:** source artifacts are content-addressed, timestamped, assigned stable entity/security IDs, version and release/availability times; normalized observations carry period, unit and provenance. Reject missing reliable availability times for strict historical evaluations; report excluded coverage. Company filings can be paired with acceptance timestamps, but acceptance is not necessarily exact public dissemination; use documented conservative release latency and test alternatives.
2. **Twin:** per issuer, filter latent fundamentals from all observations actually available as of cutoff; expose state uncertainty and staleness. Keep deterministic accounting identities distinct from statistically fitted relationships. Update online without using future releases.
3. **Scenario engine:** accept an explicit estimand, intervention, graph/version, assumptions and evidence. Validate support and constraints; simulations return model-conditional distributions or assumption-dependent sensitivity ranges. Unsupported causal claims trigger abstention or descriptive scenario labeling.
4. **Agent research:** independently issue structured claims and distributions, link spans and assumptions to evidence. Preserve original and revised outputs, contradictions, and dissent even if an aggregate is supplied. Validator tests existence, timestamps, units and quote matching; semantic entailment requires independently blinded human review, not merely citations.
5. **Uncertainty:** sample process/observation noise and parameter posterior under stated models; compare model/graph alternatives and stress assumptions separately. Apply a separately calibrated predictive interval only to the factual outcome task, unless interventions have independent interventional calibration data.
6. **Accountability:** register and seal forecasts before outcome availability; resolve against a predeclared observable target vintage and issue signed, append-only scoring and correction events. Monitoring never retroactively edits a forecast or test result.

## 4. Formal specification

### 4.1 Temporal state and observations

Let entity \(i\), forecast origin \(t\), horizon \(h\), and \(\mathcal F_t\) be the information actually available by \(t\). Each observation \(d=(i,k,[s,e],v,u,a,r,p)\) contains metric, economic period, value, unit, availability timestamp \(a\), revision ID \(r\), and source pointer \(p\). The as-of snapshot \(D_t=\{d:a\le t\}\) selects the most recently available comparable revision per entity/concept/period, preserving originals. A manifest also fixes the source snapshot and conservative ingestion latency. A missing or unknown \(a\) is not inferred from period end.

For a simple matched baseline and proposed twin, let latent state \(x_{i,t}\) include log revenue level/trend, margin and leverage, where available. At release/event steps:

\[
 x_{i,t}=A_{r_t}x_{i,t-1}+B_{r_t}z_t+\eta_{i,t},\quad
 y_{i,t}^{(k)}=H_k x_{i,t}+\epsilon_{i,t}^{(k)}.
\]

Here \(z_t\) contains only known macro/market covariates, \(r_t\) is an optional regime, \(\eta,\epsilon\) have fitted heavy-tailed or Gaussian laws, and missing observations are skipped, not set to zero. Filter \(p(x_{i,t},\theta\mid D_t)\) sequentially; parameters are fitted only on the permitted training window. Adaptive arm updates parameters on a preregistered schedule with a forgetting factor selected on validation; static arm fixes parameters after the initial fit but filters the same observations. Forecast \(F_{i,t,h}(y)=\int P(Y_{i,t+h}\le y\mid x,\theta)\,p(dx,d\theta\mid D_t)\). A deterministic transformation reconciles accounting relationships where the necessary inputs are observed; it must not manufacture unavailable fundamentals. This state model is predictive, **not automatically causal**.

### 4.2 Evidence-constrained scenario and counterfactuals

Register a time-unrolled acyclic SCM \(M=(G,\{f_j\},P(U),\theta)\), with \(V_{j,\tau}=f_j(V_{\mathrm{pa}(j),\tau},U_{j,\tau};\theta_j)\). Temporal cycles are unrolled across lags; contemporaneous cycles require an explicitly identified equilibrium model and are out of initial scope. An interventional estimand is \(\psi(M,a)=E_M[Y_{t+h}\mid do(A_t=a),D_t]\). A **retrospective individual counterfactual** additionally requires abduction of \(P(U\mid\text{observed history})\), action by replacing the relevant structural equation, and prediction with the same abducted exogenous variables; do not conflate this with an unconditional forward intervention.

Define the admissible set \(\mathcal M(D_t;\Lambda)\) using predeclared graph candidates, parameter confidence/credible regions estimated only from \(D_t\), unit/accounting constraints, empirical support rules, and sensitivity parameters \(\Lambda\) for unobserved confounding and model misspecification. Evidence references justify restrictions but do **not** establish causal validity. Report \([\inf_{M\in\mathcal M}\psi(M,a),\sup_{M\in\mathcal M}\psi(M,a)]\), with the fitted model's Monte Carlo predictive distribution shown separately. These are **assumption-dependent sensitivity ranges**, not automatically sharp identified bounds or confidence intervals. They become partial-identification bounds only if the candidate set is proven to contain all observationally compatible models under explicitly defensible assumptions and the optimization is valid. Numerical searches yield approximations, not certified extrema, unless solver guarantees are available.

Scenario gate checks: all inputs available by \(t\); evidence pointer and intervention units valid; feasible values/accounting identities; support diagnostics on the parent covariates and treatment; graph/assumption sign-off; extrapolation warning. Observational data alone generally cannot identify the effect without causal assumptions (e.g. consistency, exchangeability conditional on sufficient confounders, positivity, correct temporal model); cite them per query. Empty candidate set -> `INCONSISTENT_CONSTRAINTS`; absent causal justification or severe lack of support -> `ABSTAIN_CAUSAL`, optionally output a clearly labeled hypothetical sensitivity exercise. A contemporaneous interest-rate shock and an issuer response are not assumed exogenous merely because an analyst requested them.

### 4.3 Agent disagreement and aggregation

For each question \(q\), run \(K=3\) roles independently (fundamental, market/macro, skeptical auditor) on the **same frozen evidence set** for the primary study; role prompts are frozen. Each produces distribution \(F_k\), a concise argument, linked evidence, assumptions and abstention status. A subsequent single debate round broadcasts the other validated original claims; store \(F_k^{(0)}\) and \(F_k^{(1)}\) plus revision reason. Preserve original messages verbatim regardless of whether retained for broadcasting; separate the proposed archive from DAR's selective propagation. Where at least two valid agents provide full comparable distributions, a preregistered equal-weight mixture \(F_{mix}=K_v^{-1}\sum_{k\in V}F_k^{(1)}\) is an optional forecast, **not** a replacement for dissent. No uncalibrated self-reported confidence weighting. If fewer than two valid distributions, abstain from aggregation and report agent-level outputs.

On a shared numerical event \(E\), measure disagreement \(D_q=\frac{2}{K_v(K_v-1)}\sum_{j<k}|p_j(E)-p_k(E)|\); report its before/after change, error correlations, dissent retention and scoring. Disagreement is not intrinsically beneficial. LLMs do not constitute independent experts merely because roles differ; model pretraining and sources create correlated errors. Claim citation validity is a separate audit measure from forecast correctness.

### 4.4 Uncertainty and scoring

Sample-based predictive variance may be decomposed **within a chosen model** via total variance: \(\mathrm{Var}(Y\mid D)=E_{\theta,x}[\mathrm{Var}(Y\mid\theta,x,D)]+\mathrm{Var}_{\theta,x}(E[Y\mid\theta,x,D])\). Label the first process/observation and second parameter/state components; model/graph and scenario ambiguity are separate sensitivity analyses, not a provably exhaustive variance decomposition. Sampling error and misspecification remain.

For factual \(h\)-step forecasts, calculate past matured nonconformity scores \(s_j=|Y_{j+h}-\hat y_{j+h\mid j}|\) using only out-of-sample predictions and outcomes available by \(t\). On the last \(m\) eligible scores, use order statistic \(k=\lceil(m+1)(1-\alpha)\rceil\); if \(k>m\), abstain or return an unbounded interval. The rolling interval is \([\hat y-q_k,\hat y+q_k]\). This is a pragmatic rolling calibration, not a finite-sample coverage guarantee under serial dependence or drift. Track 80% and 90% separately; conformalized intervals need not define a full CDF and are not scored by CRPS as if they did. ACI and SPCI are separate baselines with their own prescribed assumptions.

Given full CDF \(F\) and observed \(y\), \(\mathrm{CRPS}(F,y)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}(F(z)-\mathbf 1\{y\le z\})^2 dz\); smaller is better. Binary \(\mathrm{Brier}(p,o)=(p-o)^2\). Interval coverage is mean \(\mathbf 1\{l\le y\le u\}\), width is mean \(u-l\), and the \((1-\alpha)\) interval score is \((u-l)+2(l-y)\mathbf 1\{y<l\}/\alpha+2(y-u)\mathbf 1\{y>u\}/\alpha\). Also report PIT where a continuous CDF exists, reliability plots and lower-tail exceedances. Never optimize width without coverage or hide abstentions.

## 5. Experimental protocol

### 5.1 Data and targets

**Primary H1/H4 task:** predict the next fiscal quarter's reported revenue in USD, origin at 16:30 America/New_York on the first trading day after each prior quarter's filing is publicly available. Target is the first eligible 10-Q/10-K filing's comparable quarterly revenue for the next fiscal quarter, not today's restated value. Where annual reports provide year-to-date rather than discrete Q4, derive Q4 only from contemporaneously available comparable cumulative figures, mark derivation and version; otherwise exclude and report. Target resolves on public availability of that filing, not period end. Use accession-linked SEC filings/XBRL and company submissions; map unit, fiscal period and taxonomy explicitly. Do not fill missing labels from later restatements. Coverage and issuer exclusions are tabulated by time and sector.

Universe: all eligible US exchange-listed nonfinancial operating issuers in a **historical point-in-time security master** with 8 earlier eligible quarterly revenue observations, including subsequent delistings; issuer-to-security mappings and historical membership are time-versioned. SEC documents alone do not supply historical membership or delisting-inclusive adjusted price data: acquire a licensed point-in-time security master and corporate-action/total-return series before running. If unavailable, use a disclosed fixed nonrepresentative cohort for a feasibility study only; do not claim market-wide performance. ALFRED/FRED vintage macro data may supplement features only with recorded vintage and release time. Data access, license, exact extract and archival feasibility are gating items, not assumed facts.

**Secondary market task:** 20-trading-day total return from the close of the first trading day after issuance to the close 20 trading days later, including documented corporate actions; ensure issuance precedes the entry price. This is a forecast target, not a simulated executable portfolio. Delisting cash proceeds and missing prices require source-defined handling; unresolved cases remain in the denominator and are reported, not silently dropped. For any future trading study, preregister execution delay, costs, spread, slippage, liquidity, borrow, corporate actions, turnover and a no-trade baseline.

**Causal task:** synthetic known-truth time-unrolled SCMs with both observed and hidden confounding, structural breaks, evidence errors, and interventions inside/outside support. A minimum benchmark uses independent seeds to generate 200 time steps from \(C_t=0.6C_{t-1}+U^C_t\), \(A_t=0.5C_t+U^A_t\), and \(Y_t=0.4Y_{t-1}+\beta A_t+0.7C_t+U^Y_t\), with independent standard-normal innovations, \(\beta=0.5\), and a separate hidden-confounder variant adding a common latent cause of \(A_t,Y_t\). The intervention sets \(A_t=a\); the known one-step mean effect versus \(a=0\) is \(\beta a\) in the correctly specified generator. Vary intervention size, confounder observability, evidence constraint corruption and a predeclared coefficient break; use independent simulation seeds for training and held-out test. Freeze exact ranges, number of seeds and Monte Carlo precision in preregistration. Held-out real-world interventions may be explored only where a credible external identification design and placebo checks exist. Real-world counterfactual ground truth is not observable; predictive backtests cannot validate causal effects.

### 5.2 Splits, controls and baselines

Proposed fixed calendar: development 2010–2017, validation 2018–2020, locked historical test 2021–2024, **prospective** forward test from 1 January 2027 through 31 December 2028, subject to preregistration before 2027. If preregistration occurs later, shift the prospective start forward before seeing outcomes and document the amendment. Select hyperparameters, templates and thresholds on development/validation only; test is run once after lock. Test origins are chronological. At each origin, fit/update only on released information and matured training labels; forecasts overlapping a validation/test boundary are purged if their label was not available before the next split. No globally computed standardization, cross-sectional future features, later revisions, survivorship filtering, or imputed public timestamps. Test-period online updates use only earlier resolved test outcomes and follow the identical fixed schedule in all online arms; this is prequential evaluation, not a frozen-parameter test. Keep a second no-update sensitivity analysis.

For LLM tests, historical text may be present in model pretraining, weights or retrieval caches even with strict RAG cutoffs. Record model provider, immutable version, release date, knowledge cutoff if disclosed, prompts and retrieved context; perform contamination probes but do not treat negative probes as proof. Historical LLM results are exploratory unless a demonstrably pre-cutoff model and artifact are available. The prospective test is the primary agent comparison; forbid external web access and use frozen as-of evidence.

Baselines (same eligible origins, inputs, target and evaluation; report abstention and cost):
- Revenue: seasonal naive (last year's same quarter) with residual empirical distribution; static and rolling Gaussian/heavy-tailed state-space models; regularized lagged regression and a predeclared probabilistic gradient-boosting/quantile model if feasible. All train within the same temporal constraints.
- Return task: zero mean with historical return distribution; volatility model such as GARCH with a declared innovation distribution; same cost and data rules.
- Causal: oracle SCM on simulation only, noncausal regression, unconstrained fitted SCM, and a standard sensitivity/partial-identification method with identical graph class. Include intentionally misspecified constraint sets.
- Agents: single agent; independent equal-weight ensemble without debate; conventional bull/bear debate; diversity-aware retention (DAR, implemented from the cited paper where feasible); proposed preservation protocol. Match model versions, evidence, total tokens and calls as closely as possible, report actual cost and deviations. For confidence variants use externally calibrated probabilities fit only in validation.
- Intervals: uncalibrated model quantiles, rolling residual intervals, ACI and SPCI using the same point model and mature residuals. Record implementations and parameter selection.
- Accountability: ordinary structured logs versus sealed event ledger, same forecasts and fault-injection cases. No claim of forecasting improvement from logging alone.

Ablations isolate adaptive vs static state updates, gate on/off with identical SCM, dissent archive/decision protocol vs debate alone, calibrator on/off, and ledger controls on/off. Use factorial combinations only where compute permits and preregister interactions; do not attribute full-system gains to a single component without the corresponding ablation.

### 5.3 Falsifiable hypotheses and decision rules

All directional improvements are relative to specified matched baselines, on a common eligible set. These thresholds are **proposed minimum effects**, not empirically established facts. Freeze numerical design after validation feasibility and power analysis, before unlocking historical test or prospectively issuing forecasts.

| ID | Hypothesis, estimand and comparator | Primary criterion and failure interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| H1 adaptive twin | Mean next-quarter revenue CRPS per issuer-origin for adaptive vs matched static state-space filter. | ≥2% relative reduction in pooled mean CRPS and one-sided 95% dependence-aware CI lower bound for absolute improvement >0. Otherwise no evidence of useful incremental adaptation. |
| H2 evidence constraints | On synthetic interventions, mean absolute error of \(E[Y\mid do(a)]\) vs known truth: gated SCM vs same unconstrained fitted SCM, on predeclared supported queries. | ≥10% lower error and one-sided 95% CI >0 improvement; also publish sensitivity-range coverage, width, and abstention on **all** queries. A trivial abstain-all system cannot pass; require ≥80% answered on supported cases and separately quantify false acceptance of unsupported cases. |
| H3 dissent protocol | Prospective revenue-event binary Brier (event: next quarter revenue exceeds comparable prior-year quarter, first-reported) for preserved-dissent protocol vs equal-weight independent ensemble. | ≥2% lower mean Brier with one-sided 95% CI >0 improvement, ≥90% agent participation, and full reporting of failed calls/cost. If only auditability improves, do not claim improved forecasting. |
| H4 calibration | 90% factual revenue intervals: ACI applied to the matched twin vs its uncalibrated model quantiles, with ACI step size selected on validation. | Absolute marginal coverage error reduced by ≥3 percentage points, one-sided 95% CI >0, with mean width no more than 20% larger; report interval score and regime/sector coverage. No guaranteed conditional coverage claim. |
| H5 accountability | Randomized fault-injection evaluation of ledger vs ordinary structured logs on identical prerecorded forecast jobs. | ≥15 percentage-point absolute increase in audit completeness, with one-sided 95% CI >0. Audit completeness means all preregistered fields plus replayable source/model/config hashes, issuance before outcome availability, and resolution linkage verified by a blinded auditor. It is not a predictive-accuracy claim. |

**Statistical analysis:** primary unit is issuer-origin for H1/H3/H4 and scenario replication for H2, paired on the same units. Aggregate issuer loss differentials by calendar quarter, then use a moving-block bootstrap over quarters (block length four, 10,000 resamples), retaining all issuers within sampled blocks, to account for common market shocks and serial dependence; sensitivity checks vary blocks to 2 and 8 quarters and use issuer-cluster analyses. H2 resamples independent simulation seeds/scenarios, not individual Monte Carlo draws. H5 randomizes paired jobs across arms and resamples independent fault scenarios; multiple faults per scenario stay together. Relative change uses baseline mean on identical valid cases; publish absolute differences and CIs too. Holm-adjust one-sided p-values across H1–H5 at familywise 0.05; report unadjusted CIs as descriptive and adjusted decisions separately. H4's width and H2's answer-rate restrictions are co-primary feasibility gates; failure of either means hypothesis fails. Preregister handling of missing/abstained outputs: report coverage by arm, analyze common set for paired scores, and run a separate all-origin evaluation using a fixed predeclared baseline fallback forecast for abstentions. No selective exclusions after outcomes.

Perform simulation-based power analysis on development/validation only, preserving issuer cross-sectional dependence, realistic quarterly counts, and proposed test effects. Record assumed variance, block structure, number of effective independent quarters, expected missingness, seed and 80% power target before lock. Two prospective years may be underpowered for quarterly clustered inference: if insufficient, extend the prospective window *before unblinding*, report achievable precision, and label H3 inconclusive rather than treating thousands of correlated issuer rows as independent. Unexpected source discontinuities and any protocol change require dated, reasoned amendments before seeing affected outcomes.

## 6. Database and provenance design

Use PostgreSQL plus immutable object storage for artifacts and sampled distributions. All timestamps UTC, with original source timezone retained; entities have stable IDs, not tickers as primary keys. Logical schema (PK underlined in implementation, FKs enforced):

| Table | Key fields and constraints |
|---|---|
| `entity`, `security`, `security_mapping` | entity/security UUIDs; effective-from/to, exchange, ticker, CIK and provenance; disallow overlapping mappings for same identifier. Preserve delistings. |
| `source_artifact` | `artifact_id`, source URI, license, `sha256`, published/accepted/available/ingested timestamps, time precision, immutable object URI; unique content/version identity. Unknown availability fails strict eligibility. |
| `observation_version` | ID, entity, metric/taxonomy, period start/end, value decimal, unit/currency, version, `available_at`, source artifact FK, derivation links; append corrections; unique entity/metric/period/source version. |
| `dataset_manifest` / `manifest_item` | ID, as-of cutoff, snapshot hash, selection rules/version, included observation/artifact IDs, exclusions hash; assert every available timestamp ≤ cutoff. |
| `model_version` / `twin_state` | model ID, code/container and parameter hashes, training cutoff, calibration version, state ID/entity/as-of, manifest FK, posterior artifact; enforce training availability ≤ cutoff. |
| `causal_model` / `scenario_run` | graph/equations/version, assumption records with evidence FKs, intervention and units, support test, candidate-set configuration, solver/status, result artifact and seed. Never promote sensitivity range to identified effect without review. |
| `research_run` / `agent_message` / `claim_evidence` | run ID, manifest FK, role/model/prompt version, round, immutable raw output hash, probabilities and abstention; many-to-many claim/evidence with span offsets and validation result. Preserve each revision. |
| `forecast` / `forecast_event` | forecast ID, entity, origin, issuance timestamp, horizon and exact target/resolution rule, CDF/sample artifact, interval level, agent/model/scenario/manifest references, idempotency key; events `ISSUED`, `CANCELLED`, `SUPERSEDED`, never update payload. |
| `outcome_version` / `resolution_event` / `score_event` | target, value, availability, source FK and revision; immutable resolution references chosen vintage and forecast; score references resolution and scoring version. Corrections append new resolution/score and preserve prior result. |
| `experiment` / `experiment_run` / `audit_event` | protocol hash, split, seed, code/data/environment hashes, costs and exclusions; append actor, action, time, payload hash and previous hash. |

**Enforcement:** database grants separate ingestion, issuance and resolver roles; deny update/delete on ledger tables, audit privileged access, hash-chain canonicalized events and periodically sign/checkpoint to independently controlled immutable storage. Hash chaining alone is not tamper-proof against a privileged actor rewriting the entire chain. Transactionally enforce unique idempotency key and event order; sealed issuance timestamp comes from a trusted server clock with synchronized monitoring, not a client. Reject issuance once the target outcome is available; cancellation does not erase it. Test materialized as-of queries against direct vintage reconstruction and release-boundary fixtures. Retention and redistribution comply with source licenses; public research packages can distribute hashes and scripts instead of restricted raw data.

## 7. API contracts (proposed, not implemented)

All `/v1` endpoints require authenticated project-scoped access, UTC RFC 3339 timestamps, explicit model/data versions and strict JSON schemas. Read access is separate from permission to issue forecasts or resolve outcomes. Amounts use decimals or decimal strings plus units; generated IDs below are illustrative only. `Idempotency-Key` is mandatory on writes; identical retry returns same response, conflicting payload returns `409`. Request/response examples are schematic, not claims that a live API exists.

### 7.1 As-of state

`GET /v1/entities/{entity_id}/state?as_of=2024-05-01T20:30:00Z&model_version=state-v1`

`200` -> `{"entity_id":"issuer-1","as_of":"2024-05-01T20:30:00Z","manifest_id":"manifest-1","model_version":"state-v1","state":{"revenue_level":{"mean":"100.0","unit":"USD_million","interval_90":["80.0","120.0"]}},"latest_source_available_at":"2024-04-30T20:00:00Z","stale":false,"warnings":[]}`. `404` unknown entity/model; `422` invalid cutoff or insufficient time metadata. State estimates are not observations.

### 7.2 Research and scenario requests

`POST /v1/research-runs` body: `{"entity_id":"issuer-1","as_of":"2024-05-01T20:30:00Z","manifest_id":"manifest-1","protocol_version":"agents-v1","question":"Will next-quarter revenue exceed the prior-year quarter?"}`. `202` -> `{"run_id":"run-1","status":"queued"}`. `GET /v1/research-runs/{run_id}` returns status, immutable per-agent first/revised messages, evidence links, claims, aggregate if eligible, abstentions and warnings. Rejected/invalid citations remain visible with status.

`POST /v1/scenario-runs` body: `{"entity_id":"issuer-1","as_of":"2024-05-01T20:30:00Z","manifest_id":"manifest-1","causal_model_version":"scm-v1","estimand":"interventional_mean_revenue","horizon_quarters":1,"intervention":{"variable":"interest_rate","operation":"set","value":"0.05","unit":"annual_fraction"},"assumption_set":"sensitivity-v1","seed":42}`. `202` returns run ID; `GET /v1/scenario-runs/{id}` returns `status`, support diagnostics, graph/assumptions/evidence, model-conditional distribution artifact, sensitivity-range endpoints and solver limitations, or a structured abstention reason. Reject invalid unit/unknown intervention with `422`; never silently substitute a predictive correlation for an intervention.

### 7.3 Forecast issuance and resolution

`POST /v1/forecasts` body: `{"entity_id":"issuer-1","origin":"2024-05-01T20:30:00Z","target":{"metric":"quarterly_revenue","period_end":"2024-06-30","unit":"USD","resolution_rule":"first_eligible_filing_v1"},"manifest_id":"manifest-1","model_version":"state-v1","distribution_artifact_id":"dist-1","interval_90":["80000000","120000000"]}`. Server verifies artifact/manifest existence, as-of and trained-before-origin rules, cutoff, target units and that target is not already public, and atomically seals issuance; `201` -> `{"forecast_id":"forecast-1","issued_at":"2024-05-01T20:31:00Z","event_hash":"sha256:...","status":"issued"}`. These dates are illustrative; historical issuance is forbidden through the live endpoint. Backtests use a separate `experiment` interface marked simulated and cannot masquerade as prospective forecasts.

`POST /v1/forecasts/{id}/resolutions` is resolver-only, body `{"outcome_version_id":"outcome-1","resolution_rule":"first_eligible_filing_v1"}`; `201` returns `resolution_event_id`, selected vintage, score event IDs and outcome availability time. `409` if not yet eligible, previously resolved without a correction event, target mismatch, or idempotency conflict. `GET /v1/forecasts/{id}/audit` returns the full immutable event chain, hashes, linked evidence/model, resolution and corrections with authorization-filtered artifact references. Common error: `{"error":{"code":"ABSTAIN_CAUSAL","message":"Identification assumptions not approved","details":{}}}`; also use `401/403`, `404`, `409`, `422`, `429`, and `503` as appropriate. Define pagination, versioning, rate limits and signed artifact URLs in an OpenAPI schema before deployment.

## 8. Implementation plan and acceptance gates

0. **Research registration and feasibility:** complete deeper review, data-license and availability audit, exact cohorts, simulation-based power analysis and a timestamped preregistration; inventory required resources. **Gate:** evidence and feasible sources for as-of/delistings, or explicitly restrict to a feasibility cohort.
1. **Data foundation:** artifact/vintage ingestion, security master, release-time mapping, normalization, validation and manifests. **Gate:** automated tests for restatements, after-close filing, fiscal-quarter conversions, time zones, corporate actions, delistings and no look-ahead; publish missingness and source coverage.
2. **Forecast baselines and twin:** seasonal naive, regression and static/adaptive state models, probability distributions and walk-forward runner. **Gate:** deterministically replay a frozen manifest and beat no baseline by assertion alone; demonstrate paired backtests and report negative outcomes.
3. **SCM/simulation research:** generate benchmark with known interventions, implement model registry, gate and sensitivity optimizer. **Gate:** recover oracle results on correctly specified toy SCM; detect deliberately incompatible constraints; measure misspecified-constraint harm and abstentions. Never infer causal validation from an observational score.
4. **Agent study:** structured outputs, evidence linking, original/revised messages, matched baselines and manual blinded evidence audit. **Gate:** replay stored outputs, handle missing/invalid agents, measure costs and contamination risk; freeze prompts before prospective trial.
5. **Calibration and accountability:** rolling/ACI/SPCI comparison and ledger, resolver, scoring. **Gate:** maturity-only residuals; reproducible scoring and fault-injection validation; append-only corrections and authorization tests.
6. **Locked test, then prospective study:** freeze code/config/analysis, run held-out evaluations once and publish all results, failures and resource use; prospective issuance begins only after preregistration. **Gate:** independent replication and model-risk review before any production use.

Suggested stack: Python statistical/ML research environment, PostgreSQL, object storage, a typed HTTP service, and a deterministic workflow scheduler; exact libraries/versions pinned after feasibility checks. No external integration, actual user data, live deployment or trading is required to validate the proposal. Document budget, hardware, staff and compute during preregistration; defer expensive agent runs until baseline and leakage gates pass.

## 9. Reproducibility, governance and risks

- Publish the protocol and amendment history before test; register hypotheses, target definitions, exclusions, metrics, comparison and multiplicity. Maintain versioned code, dependency lockfiles, container digest, random seeds, hardware, library/model versions, provider and decoding settings, prompts, replayable agent responses, data licenses, data hashes, extraction scripts, snapshots/manifest hashes, source availability and cleaning/identity rules. Keep an auditable environment and independent reproduction instructions. Exact LLM outputs may not replay with hosted nondeterministic providers; archive outputs and distinguish result replay from model regeneration.
- Record every run, failure, timeout, abstention, token usage, latency, cost, human annotation guidance, reviewer agreement and data revisions. Share public artifacts or legally redistributable synthetic substitutes. Pin provenance to every chart and statistic.
- **Model/data risk:** accounting comparability, fiscal-calendar changes, restatements, missing/delisted prices, corporate actions, reporting lag, issuer/security mapping and regime changes can dominate observed gains. Quarantine ambiguous cases, disclose coverage, and monitor shift; do not silently forward-fill labels.
- **Causal risk:** unmeasured confounding, simultaneous reactions, weak support, wrong graph and equilibrium feedback invalidate causal interpretations; an evidence link is not causal identification. Show competing assumptions, bounds/sensitivity and abstain when unsupported.
- **Agent risk:** hallucinated or misleading citations, correlated errors, prompt injection in filings and unstable hosted models. Treat retrieved text as untrusted data, sandbox tools, restrict egress and secrets, verify evidence pointers, and have blinded human auditors assess entailment. Preserve dissent without presenting it as necessarily correct.
- **Uncertainty risk:** calibration can break during shifts, on subgroups or tail events. Display interval coverage/width by period, sector and regime with counts and uncertainty; alert on deterioration without using alerts to alter a locked evaluation retrospectively.
- **Security/legal:** enforce least privilege, project isolation, encrypted transport/storage, retention rules and licensed-data controls; do not ingest material nonpublic information or personal data by default. This is research, not regulated advice; no guarantee of returns, causal validity or financial suitability. Independent legal and model-risk review is required before operational financial use.

## 10. Expected outputs, including null results

A complete study releases the review and search log, gap matrix, preregistration, versioned schema/API and reproducible code, a dataset/vintage coverage report, baseline and ablation tables, scoring and calibration plots, synthetic intervention and abstention results, dissent/claim-audit analysis, ledger fault tests, uncertainty intervals and full cost accounting. Findings must distinguish historical from prospective evidence, synthetic from real interventions, predictive from causal claims, and established methods from any newly validated contribution. An inconclusive or negative outcome is explicitly reported rather than reclassified as success.
